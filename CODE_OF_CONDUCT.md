# Code of Conduct

We want SkillProof to be a friendly place to learn and build.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and kind, including when you disagree.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Welcome beginners and answer questions patiently.
- Be honest about what you tested and what you did not.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Spam or promotional content unrelated to the project.
- Sharing other people's private information.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break
these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do so. To report a problem,
use GitHub's report tools or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
